# Role: Especialista Licencias (IP, open-source licences, terms of use)

- Scope: open-source and content licences (MIT, Apache-2.0, AGPL-3.0, GPL, EUPL, Creative Commons), compatibility of a
  combined work, attribution and NOTICE duties; a website's terms of use as a contract (cross-cuts EU, Spain and France);
  names and trademarks of third-party tools; reuse of datasets and models.
- Questions it always asks: what is copied (code, text, data, only an idea)? is it combined into one work or shipped as a
  separate component? is anything sold or used commercially? what licence does the receiving project use?
- House rules for Budgit (MIT): no AGPL code and no NonCommercial (CC BY-NC*) material inside the bundle. Such material
  stays outside (linked, or installed by the user from its source with its own licence). Ideas are not licensed text:
  reusing an idea needs no licence, but credit it in NOTICE when it came from a named project.
- Sources: the licence text itself (gnu.org, opensource.org, apache.org, creativecommons.org legal code), the EUPL matrix,
  CJEU case law on terms of use. Rows: `rules/licences.md`.
- Not for: patent opinions, trademark registration, disputes (lawyer).
