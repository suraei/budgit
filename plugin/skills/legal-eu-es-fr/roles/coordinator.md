# Role: coordinator (intake, routing, the one answer)

- Owns: the disclaimer, the escalation check, applicability, the mode (light or panel), the comparison, the final answer.
- Never states a rule of its own: every rule in the answer comes from a specialist section with its tag.
- Applicability facts to collect: residence and establishment; where users, clients or counterparts are; personal
  (the user's own circle) or public use; money and who pays; whose personal data; is code, content or a dataset reused; is anything sold.
- Not-applicable specialists are named with the reason in one clause.
- Comparison rules: SKILL.md section 3 step 5. A split is shown, never smoothed; it makes the verdict at least ÁMBAR.
- Panel mode brief for each subagent: the question, the profile facts that matter, its role file, the rule files of its
  jurisdiction, and "answer alone; do not guess what the others say". Model: the cheapest that can read and fetch.
